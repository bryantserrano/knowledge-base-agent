INK & ANCHOR TATTOO STUDIO — CUSTOMER FAQs

Q: Where is the studio located?
A: Ink & Anchor Tattoo Studio is located at 742 Sterling Avenue, Suite 100.

Q: What are your working hours?
A: Monday through Friday: 10:00 AM–7:00 PM. Saturday: 11:00 AM–5:00 PM. Sunday: closed. All hours are in the America/Mexico_City time zone.

Q: How much is the booking deposit?
A: The deposit is $100 for custom pieces under 3 hours and $250 for full-day sessions. Deposits are non-refundable. Pricing for other appointment lengths is not specified here; ask the studio.

Q: Can I reschedule without losing my deposit?
A: Give at least 48 hours' notice to reschedule without losing your deposit.

Q: Can I cancel and get my deposit back?
A: Deposits are non-refundable. The documented option for keeping your deposit is rescheduling with at least 48 hours' notice.

Q: What happens if I arrive late?
A: There is a 15-minute grace period. If you arrive more than 20 minutes late without notice, you lose your deposit and the artist may no longer be available for your session. The policy does not specify what happens between 15 and 20 minutes; ask the studio.

Q: What happens if I miss my appointment without notice?
A: No-shows lose access to online booking and must pay 100% of the missed session's estimated cost upfront to rebook.

Q: Where can I get aftercare guidance?
A: Ask your artist for the studio's aftercare instructions for your tattoo and dressing. This assistant cannot assess healing, diagnose a condition, or provide personalized medical advice.

Q: Are touch-ups included?
A: Free touch-ups are offered within the first 90 days, except for tattoos on fingers, hands, feet, or inner lips, which are excluded from free touch-ups.

Q: Can I book through this chat?
A: This chat answers questions only. It cannot book, cancel, or reschedule appointments. Contact the studio directly; a booking link, phone number, and email address are not provided in these FAQs.

Q: Do you accept walk-ins, tattoo minors, or offer a specific tattoo style?
A: These details are not provided in the FAQs. Contact the studio to confirm.
